# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free
community for everyone who participates in this project.

Contributors are expected to communicate constructively, assume good intent,
focus on the technical issue, and accept different viewpoints. Personal
attacks, discrimination, harassment, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive
behavior are not acceptable.

Report a concern privately through the repository owner, [tech-zjf](https://github.com/tech-zjf),
or GitHub's private repository reporting tools. Maintainers will handle reports
confidentially and fairly where possible.
